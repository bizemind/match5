Which colour do you want to make?

Target colour

Have a physical colour chip or swatch? A handheld colour sensor can read it and give you a hex code. The Nix Mini works well on fabric and paint surfaces — press it against your chip, copy the hex, and paste it above.

Available pigments

Fevicryl colours only. Every recipe is made from these five: Chrome Yellow, Magenta and Cerulean Blue give the hues; Black deepens and greys; White softens and tints.

Enter a hex code or colour name above to get a recipe.

Sized to your 15 ml bottles: the most-used colour is the full 15 ml, the rest scale to match. Mix pigments in thoroughly and check in natural light. Colours may vary by paint and surface.
